✦ THE CLEVER BIT

How RAG finds
an answer.

RAG means Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The app searches the book first, then gives the useful passages to its local AI model.

1❝

You ask

You ask something in your own words.

IN · Your question

2⌕

Find passages

Semantic search and word matching help find relevant parts of the book.

OUT · Evidence passages

3✧

AI reads

A reader model picks an answer span. FLAN-T5 uses the question and passage to phrase a reply.

IN · Question + evidence

4▤

Answer + source

You get a response with a chapter passage you can open and check.

OUT · Answer + citation

✦

A LITTLE EXAMPLE FROM OZ

“What does the Scarecrow ask Oz to give him?”

Search finds passages about the Scarecrow and the Wizard. The reader selects evidence from one passage; the local language model uses that evidence to phrase an answer.

What if the book doesn’t say? The app is designed to say it couldn’t find enough evidence instead of making up a book answer.
